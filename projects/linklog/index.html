LinkLog

(Service Design, UX Prototype)

A browser-based service dashboard prototype for community reporting workflows, designed around local files, follow-up tracking, and de-identified seasonal exports.

Year 2026

Role UX Research / Service Design / Local Web Prototype

Tools Figma, HTML, CSS, JavaScript

Methods Workflow mapping, service blueprinting, usability testing, prototyping

Problem Staff needed a clearer way to manage local client records, follow-ups, and seasonal reporting without technical file-handling confusion.

Process Workflow mapping → Onboarding redesign → Reporting flow → Prototype testing

Notes

The workflow needed more orientation.

The project focused on onboarding, role selection, folder guidance, plain-language UX copy, and reporting previews while keeping sensitive source material out of the portfolio.

Work

Mapped the service and prototyped the tool.

I translated the workflow into a browser prototype with role-based onboarding, dashboard states, overdue follow-up cues, and de-identified export previews.

Role selection
Dashboard
Export preview

Key decisions

Plain language first.

Setup instructions were written as operational guidance rather than technical documentation. Reporting flows foregrounded de-identified reports and sample records.

01Reframed setup around staff roles instead of technical file structure.

02Moved report configuration into a preview-first flow.

03Used plain-language labels to reduce uncertainty for non-technical users.

Outcome

The workflow became easier to follow.

The final prototype made the reporting workflow easier to follow by making each step visible, named, and reversible.
