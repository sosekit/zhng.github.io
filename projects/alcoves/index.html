Alcoves

(Interior Design, Product Design, Spatial Design)

A modular seating system exploring personalized thermal comfort, material interaction, and human-centered spatial experience.

Year 2024

Role Physical UX / Service Design / Spatial Design

Tools AutoCAD, Rhino 3D, physical prototyping

Methods Material research, comfort research, prototyping, observational feedback

Problem Comfort in shared seating environments is often treated as fixed, even though people experience temperature, material, and control differently.

Process Research → Material studies → Prototype → Observation → Exhibition

Notes

Shared environments often treat comfort as uniform.

The project asks how a spatial system can support individual comfort preferences through material, enclosure, and interaction.

Work

Researched material behavior and prototyped a spatial system.

The project included comfort research, material studies, physical prototyping, observational feedback, and exhibition presentation.

Material study
Prototype
Interaction model

Key decisions

Comfort became the interface.

Form, material, and enclosure were treated as feedback systems that could communicate affordance without a screen.

01Used material response as a way to communicate comfort options.

02Designed enclosure as a controllable service experience, not just furniture.

03Framed observation as feedback for spatial interaction.

Outcome

Spatial design can behave like UX.

The project clarified how physical environments can communicate state, control, and service experience through subtle material signals.
