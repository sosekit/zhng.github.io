Sparkbook

(Mobile UX, App Design)

A personal landmarks app for saving, organizing, and sharing meaningful places with close friends.

Year 2026

Role Mobile UX / Interaction Design / Figma Prototype

Tools Figma

Methods Wireframing, user flows, prototyping, usability feedback

Problem Users needed a more personal way to save, organize, and share meaningful places beyond generic bookmarking.

Process Concept → User flows → Wireframes → Prototype → Usability feedback

Notes

Discovery can become too public too quickly.

The design focused on navigation clarity, list creation, bookmark interactions, and quieter sharing with selected audiences.

Work

Mapped flows and iterated the prototype.

I designed user flows, wireframes, and prototype iterations shaped by usability feedback around saving, organizing, and sharing landmarks.

List creation
Bookmark state
Sharing flow

Key decisions

Sharing became selective by default.

The experience emphasized close-friend sharing and audience choice rather than broad public posting.

01Separated saving from sharing so users could collect places privately first.

02Kept list creation lightweight and close to the landmark detail view.

03Used audience selection to make social discovery feel personal.

Outcome

Personal context is the product.

The prototype became clearer once landmarks were treated less like content and more like small personal records with context, ownership, and audience.
